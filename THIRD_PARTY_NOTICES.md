# Third-party notices

The bundled TypeSafe AI development skill in `.agents/skills/typesafe-ai/`
comes from `typesafe-ai/skills` and retains its
[MIT notice](.agents/skills/typesafe-ai/LICENSE), copyright 2026 TypeSafe AI.
`skills-lock.json` records the source and imported content hash.

Dependencies retain their own licenses. `pnpm-lock.yaml` records the application
dependency versions; OpenPresence's MIT license does not replace dependency
notices. Preserve their notices when distributing installed packages or binaries.

Raw third-party social captures and private development evidence are excluded
from the public source distribution. Synthetic test fixtures and demo interface
screenshots are included for development and documentation.
